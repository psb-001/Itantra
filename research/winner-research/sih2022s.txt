No idea is too small Agriculture and Rural development Designing devices keeping in mind the need to enhance the primary sector of India - Agriculture and the lives of our Rural Population. Clean water Creating state-of-the-art devices to improve distribution, management and purification of water. Energy/renewable energy Innovative ideas that help manage and generate renewable sources more efficiently. Finance Generating ideas and solutions that will simplify and ease the task in the financial field. Food technology Creating state of art solutions to manage and process our agriculture produce. Healthcare and bio-medical devices Designing devices that would help in managing healthcare better. Life sciences Ideas for improvement in the field of life sciences and related sectors. Miscellaneous Technology ideas in tertiary sectors like Hospitality, Services, Entertainment, Tourism and Retail. Mobile and web development Idea implementation by web and mobile app development technology. Robotics and drones There is a need to design drones and robots that can solve some of the pressing challenges of India such as handling medical emergencies, search and rescue operations, etc. Security and surveillance Creating state-of-the-art safety and security technologies for India. Smart cities Designing devices for the betterment of the tribal population. Smart communication Designing devices which would help in easing communication channels between various communication devices and points. Smart textiles Technology related to textiles that can sense and respond to changes in their environment and are classified fields of applications such as medical, transportation and energy and protection, security communication and textile electronics. Smart vehicles Creating intelligent devices to improve commutation facilities, quality of travel experience and overall travel safety features. Sports and fitness Creating intelligent devices to improve the health and sport sector. Sustainable environment Innovative ideas that help manage environment   and  sources more efficiently. Waste management Solutions could be in the form of waste segregation, disposal, and improved transportation system.
