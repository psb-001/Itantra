## What HECN actually is (from the deck)

**HECN = "Lifeline in Blackouts"** — a dual-layer, infrastructure-independent emergency mesh:

- **Layer 1:** Short-range **BLE mesh** — trapped victims broadcast SOS from personal phones; packets hop across nearby civilians' phones ("living repeaters", self-healing chain)
- **Layer 2:** **Long-range LoRa** (~10 km) — fixed solar-powered nodes (ESP32 + Semtech SX1278, LiFePO4 battery) capture BLE packets and rebroadcast to a rescue base
- **Command side:** React + Mapbox real-time dashboard, urgency coding (high/med/low), filter by area, assign teams, acknowledgement tracking; GPS plotted to ~20 m
- **Claimed unit cost:** "< ₹2,000/node"; claimed 10+ km LoRa range
- **Extra claims:** civilian phones as repeaters without app install; peace-time dual use (air quality / flood / gas-leak sensing on streetlight-mounted nodes)

## Comparison 1 — SIH26173 (ISRO, Software, 8/500)
*"iTantra: Indian Multilingual TTS & STT Aided Neural Transceiver Radio Access for low bitrate links"*

| Dimension | HECN (PPT) | SIH26173 |
|---|---|---|
| Core problem | No channel at all when towers/grid die | Voice is too data-heavy for low-bitrate links |
| Proposed fix | BLE + LoRa mesh carrying **data** (SOS packets) | **Compress voice to text** (offline STT), send tiny text, resynthesise (offline TTS) |
| Category | Hardware-flavoured | **Software** (Android app) |
| Key components | ESP32, SX1278, solar, batteries | STT + TTS models, 10 languages, fully offline |
| Hardware spend | ₹8k–25k depending on node count | ~₹0 if you own two Android phones |
| AI involvement | None required | **The whole product** (accuracy = 40% of marks) |
| Judged metrics | None published (open theme) | Efficiency 20 / Accuracy 40 / Latency 20 |

**Overlap: only the scenario (distress comms on weak links). The technical mechanisms share almost nothing.**

- HECN *avoids* voice entirely — it sends tiny data packets, which is why it's cheap and low-power.
- 26173 *is about voice* — STT/TTS on-device, ten languages, push-to-talk walkie-talkie loop.
- HECN's relevant edge for 26173: **nobody** — the PPT has no speech pipeline at all.

**Fit with HECN as-is: WEAK.** To target 26173 you'd keep only the "low-bitrate link" context and rebuild the product as an offline multilingual speech app — different hardware, different skills, different risks. Your ₹8,000 budget becomes nearly irrelevant; the difficulty moves to on-device AI in 10 Indic languages.

## The three claims in the deck that must be proven (or softened) before submission
1. **"<₹2,000 per node"** — needs a real BOM with Indian retail prices; 10 nodes ≈ ₹20k. If the team cap is ₹8,000, this deck's own numbers exceed it → scope to 3–4 nodes (~₹6–8k) or fix the claim.
2. **"10+ km LoRa range"** — achievable in line-of-sight rural; realistic urban figure is far less. Demo plan must state antenna, height, and environment.
3. **"Civilian phones repeat SOS without any app"** — BLE advertisements can carry small payloads to *scanning* devices, but "every nearby phone becomes a repeater with no software" is not true on stock iOS/Android. This is the deck's most attackable claim under judging. Reframe as: app-based mesh (same vendor app installed) OR fixed repeater nodes only.
